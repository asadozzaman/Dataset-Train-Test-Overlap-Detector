How these percentages are calculated

SSIM compares local structure after EXIF orientation, grayscale conversion and resizing to 128 × 128 pixels. The reported percentage is SSIM × 100, clipped at zero, using uniform 7 × 7 windows, sample covariance, and a pixel range of 255. Perceptual agreement is the matching fraction of 64 dHash bits.

Neither score is a probability or proof of shared origin. Color changes, crop, alignment and low-detail images can produce misleading scores. The CPU checker reviews at most 2,000 images and scores at most 500 candidate pairs.
